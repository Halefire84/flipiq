## Full Description (4000 chars max, target <500 words)

```
Stop guessing. Know exactly what a flip is worth before you buy it.

FlipIQ is the profit calculator built for resellers — flea market hunters, eBay
flippers, estate sale diggers, and thrift store pickers. Enter a few numbers, get
an instant BUY, RISKY, or PASS verdict backed by real math: profit, ROI, max buy
price, and a confidence score from 0–100 based on how many comps you've seen.

No account. No internet. No eBay API key. Just numbers that tell you the truth.

**What you enter:**
- Buy price (what you'd pay)
- Sell low / sell high (your comp range)
- Platform fee % (eBay, Facebook, Mercari — whatever you sell on)
- Shipping cost
- Buyer premium % (for auction houses)
- Comps seen (how many sold listings you checked)

**What you get instantly:**
- Profit in dollars
- ROI as a percentage
- Max buy price (the most you should ever pay)
- BUY / RISKY / PASS verdict
- Confidence score (0–100) — fewer comps = lower confidence, so you know when
  to do more research before pulling the trigger

**Real use cases:**
- Standing at an estate sale, sticker says $40, you need an answer in 10 seconds
- Scanning a thrift store shelf — is this worth pulling?
- At auction — the buyer premium just changed the math, FlipIQ catches it
- Reviewing a batch of items at the end of the day before listing

**Fully offline.** No Wi-Fi required, no login, no subscription wall on the core
verdict. Works in a basement, a barn, or a storage unit with no signal.

**Free tier:** 3 scans per day — enough to test the tool on real items before
committing.

**Pro — $14.99/month:**
- Unlimited scans
- Full scan history (review every flip decision you've made)
- Built for pickers who run volume, not just weekend warriors

FlipIQ is built by CRTC AI, the same team behind Appraze. We build tools for
resellers, by people who understand the hustle.

Download free. Run the numbers. Know before you buy.
```
